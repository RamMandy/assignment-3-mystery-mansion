The Vanishing Diamond

Every clue has a story. Every suspect has a secret.

The Story

During an evening party at Blackwood Mansion, the lights suddenly go out. When they return, a priceless diamond has disappeared! Three suspects remain inside the mansion. Your mission is to uncover the truth and find the thief.

How to Play

  1. Explore the mansion and investigate suspicious locations.
  2. You have 5 investigation moves.
  3. Every new search or questioning costs 1 move.
  4. Some investigations reveal useful clues, while others are misleading.
  5. Collecting clues and returning to the hallway are free.
  6. You can search another location without leaving the same room.
  7. You can accuse a suspect at any time, even without clues.
  8. When your moves run out, you must make a final accusation.
  9. Identify the correct thief to win!
